Pilot Reader Study

Acquisition-Aware AI Reliability in CT Lung Nodule Follow-Up

What you'll do

You'll review {{ n_cases }} CT lung nodule follow-up cases from the LIDC-IDRI research dataset. For each case you'll see:

  • Prior CT — AI-detected nodule size
  • Follow-up CT — AI detection result (detected or not detected)
  • On some cases: an acquisition reliability score indicating whether the follow-up CT parameters support direct AI comparison

For each case, record your management recommendation. Total time: approximately 20–30 minutes.

Acquisition reliability scores

Some cases will show one of these flags:

GREEN — Comparable YELLOW — Reduced sensitivity RED — Unreliable comparison

Other cases will show no flag. This is part of what the study is testing — whether this information changes clinical decisions.

About the data

All cases are de-identified research data from the publicly available LIDC-IDRI dataset. No patient-identifiable information is involved. Your responses will be used solely for research purposes and stored securely.

This study uses simulated prior/follow-up pairs derived from acquisition-parameter perturbations of single-timepoint CT scans — not longitudinal patient data.
